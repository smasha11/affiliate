How to choose

Use the sink as a second counter

An over-the-sink board with a built-in colander adds a prep surface that disappears when you're done.

Nesting beats sets with stands

Measuring sets and bowls that stack into one piece save more drawer space than any stand or caddy.

Get knives off the counter

A wall-mounted magnetic strip frees the square foot a knife block covers. In a rental, use an adhesive-backed strip rated for the weight.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best cutting board for a small kitchen?

An over-the-sink cutting board, because it adds prep space instead of using it up. Pick one with an adjustable width so it fits your basin.

Are collapsible silicone colanders sturdy?

Good ones have a rigid rim and handles, so they hold a full pot of pasta. Avoid fully floppy models without a frame.

How many knives does a small kitchen need?

Three: a chef's knife, a paring knife and a serrated bread knife cover almost every task.
