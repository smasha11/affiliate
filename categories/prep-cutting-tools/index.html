How to choose

One tool instead of five

A chopper with interchangeable blades replaces a mandoline, grater, dicer and julienne peeler, all stored in one container.

Swap the dish rack for a mat

A microfiber drying mat folds away when you're done. A dish rack never leaves the counter.

Keep flat tools in the drawer

A slim digital scale and sheathed shears both store flat, so they don't need counter space at all.

Frequently asked questions

Is a vegetable chopper worth it in a small kitchen?

Yes, if it replaces other tools. A 10-in-1 chopper with slicing, grating and julienne blades does the work of several gadgets and stores in one container.

Do I need a kitchen scale?

For baking and portioning, yes. A slim digital scale weighs to the gram, takes less room than a full set of measuring cups and stores in a drawer.

How many knives does a small kitchen need?

Three: a chef's knife, a paring knife and a serrated bread knife cover almost every task.
