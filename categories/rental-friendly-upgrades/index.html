How to choose

Prep the surface before anything sticks

Clean with rubbing alcohol and let it dry. Most adhesive failures come from grease, not weak tape.

Respect the weight rating

Adhesive hooks and rails list a maximum load. Stay under it, and spread heavy things across several hooks.

Test removal somewhere hidden

Peel-and-stick products behave differently on painted drywall than on tile. Try one in a corner before covering a wall.

Frequently asked questions

Will peel-and-stick products damage rental walls?

Products with removable adhesive come off cleanly from sealed tile and most semi-gloss paint. Pull slowly at a low angle, and warm stubborn strips with a hairdryer.

How do I protect the cabinet under my sink?

A silicone under-sink mat with raised edges catches drips and small leaks before they reach the cabinet floor, one of the most common deposit deductions. It can be trimmed around pipes.

What is the cheapest rental kitchen upgrade?

A self-adhesive paper towel holder or a stainless sink caddy. Both cost under $20 and clear counter space every day.
