How to choose

Measure the opening, then subtract half an inch

Cabinet openings are narrower than the cabinet box, and hinges steal width. Measure the door opening, not the inside wall, and leave half an inch so the organizer slides in without scraping.

Go up before you go out

Vertical space is the cheapest square footage in a small kitchen. A riser shelf or a two-tier pull-out usually beats a wider bin.

Pick clear or labeled bins

If you can't see what's in a bin from the front, you'll buy a duplicate. Clear sides or a front label keep a small pantry honest.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best way to add storage to a small kitchen without remodeling?

Start with the space you're not using: an L-shaped pull-out under the sink, containers that share one footprint so they stack, and an expandable drawer organizer. None of them need tools.

Will under-sink organizers fit around plumbing?

Look for models with height-adjustable or removable shelves. Our pick has an L-shaped top basket that mounts left or right to clear the pipes. Measure from the cabinet floor to the bottom of the pipe before you buy.

Glass or plastic containers: which saves more space?

Both stack well when every container shares a footprint. Glass goes from fridge to oven to table, so it can replace a baking dish. Plastic is lighter and better for lunches.
