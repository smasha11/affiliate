How to choose

Count what it replaces

A multi-cooker that retires a rice cooker and a slow cooker frees two spots. Single-job gadgets need to clear a much higher bar.

Check the stored size, not just the running size

Removable cords, nesting lids and fold-down handles decide whether an appliance goes back in a cabinet or lives on the counter forever.

Mind your outlets

Small kitchens often have one or two outlets. Avoid running two high-wattage appliances on the same circuit, and skip anything whose cord won't reach.

Frequently asked questions

What size air fryer is best for one or two people?

A 2- to 3-quart basket handles meals for one or two and fits in most upper cabinets. Larger models rarely justify the counter space in a studio.

Can I use an induction burner in a rental?

Yes. Plug-in induction burners need no installation, and they're a common fix for two-burner apartment stoves. Check that your cookware is magnetic first.

Which small appliance saves the most space?

A mini multi-cooker, because it replaces several single-purpose appliances at once.
