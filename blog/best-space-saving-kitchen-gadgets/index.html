What we'd skip in a small kitchen

Single-use gadgets with big bases, like dedicated egg cookers and quesadilla makers, rarely earn their space. Neither do bulky countertop organizers that hold less than the drawer they replace. If a tool can't live in a cabinet, you need to use it every day.

Frequently asked questions

What kitchen gadgets save the most space?

Tools that do double duty or disappear when you're done: an over-the-sink cutting board, a collapsible colander, detachable-handle cookware and a two-tier cabinet shelf free up the most room for the money.

How do I organize a kitchen with no counter space?

Move everything you can off the counter and onto walls, cabinet doors and the sink. Use an over-the-sink board for prep, a magnetic knife strip for knives and a rolling cart in any gap beside the fridge.

Are space-saving gadgets worth it for renters?

Most are. None of the picks on this list need drilling or installation, and they move with you to the next apartment.
