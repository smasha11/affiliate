How to fit them in

Keep one appliance on the counter, the one you use daily, and give the rest a single cabinet shelf. Coil cords inside each appliance before you store it, and keep the heaviest one at waist height so you'll actually use it.

Frequently asked questions

What appliances does a studio apartment kitchen need?

Most people need three at most: a multi-cooker, an air fryer and a kettle or toaster. Add a plug-in burner if your stove has only two burners.

Where do I store appliances in a tiny kitchen?

Give them one cabinet shelf and keep only your daily appliance on the counter. Coil cords inside the appliance so they stack neatly.
