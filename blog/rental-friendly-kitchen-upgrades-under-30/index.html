How to remove everything at move-out

Warm adhesive strips with a hairdryer for 20 seconds, then pull the tab slowly and straight down along the wall, not out toward you. Leftover residue lifts with a little cooking oil on a paper towel, followed by dish soap and water.

Frequently asked questions

What can I change in a rental kitchen without losing my deposit?

Stick to upgrades that install with removable adhesive, tension or magnets: under-cabinet LED bars, adhesive rails, tension-rod dividers and peel-and-stick hooks. Keep the packaging until move-out so you know the removal method.

Do adhesive hooks hold up in a kitchen?

Yes, if you clean the surface with rubbing alcohol first and stay under the weight rating. Heat and grease weaken adhesive, so keep hooks at least a foot from the stovetop.

What's the best kitchen upgrade under $30?

Rechargeable under-cabinet LED bars. They make a dark rental kitchen safer to cook in, and they peel off cleanly.
