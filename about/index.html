About Tiny Kitchen Lab

Tiny Kitchen Lab recommends one kind of product: things that make a small kitchen work better. Everything we list is researched and tested in real apartment kitchens, not on a showroom counter.

Tiny Kitchen Lab started in a six-foot galley kitchen with two burners, one drawer and no dishwasher. Every product that promised to save space got tested there first, and most of them didn't.

So we started keeping score. Today we test gadgets, storage and tools in a rotating set of apartment kitchens, from studios to narrow rental galleys, and only recommend the ones that leave you with more room than you started with.

We earn a commission when you buy through our Amazon links. That never changes a score, and we don't accept paid placements.

How the Lab Score works

Four steps, the same for a $9 colander and a $90 multi-cooker.

  1. Research

    We read the specs, check the real dimensions and go through owner reviews, then shortlist products built for small spaces.

  2. Measure

    Each product goes into a real apartment kitchen. We measure its footprint while in use and while stored.

  3. Live with it

    We use it through at least two weeks of normal cooking. Does it hold up, and does it actually get used?

  4. Score it

    Space saved, everyday performance, build quality and value are each scored, then weighted into one Lab Score out of 10.

What the score weighs

  • Space saved40%
  • Performance30%
  • Build quality15%
  • Value15%
